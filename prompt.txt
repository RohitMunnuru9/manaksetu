2. PROBLEM
==================================================

Government departments, Public Sector Enterprises, procurement agencies and private organisations purchase products and services through tender and e-procurement systems.

While preparing a tender, procurement officers must mention the correct Indian Standards published by the Bureau of Indian Standards.

This is difficult because:

- Thousands of Indian Standards exist.
- Similar products may be covered by different standards.
- One product may require multiple connected standards.
- Standards frequently receive revisions and amendments.
- Older versions may be withdrawn or superseded.
- Standards contain normative and cross-references.
- Some products require test-method standards.
- Some products require safety or installation standards.
- Some products fall under mandatory BIS certification.
- Quality Control Orders have different enforcement dates.
- Tender descriptions can be unclear or incomplete.
- Normal keyword search cannot always understand technical meaning.
- Manual standards research takes considerable time.
- Referencing incorrect standards can cause disputes and poor-quality procurement.

The result may be:

- Missing standards
- Incorrect IS numbers
- Outdated versions
- Missing certification requirements
- Ambiguous tender specifications
- Reduced product quality
- Tender disputes
- Re-tendering
- Delayed procurement

==================================================

==================================================

ManakSetu is not a general chatbot.

The AI may:

- Understand tender descriptions
- Extract technical requirements
- Search verified records
- Rank standards
- Explain recommendations
- Suggest tender clauses

The AI must never:

- Invent an Indian Standard number
- Modify an Indian Standard number
- Guess a certification requirement
- Present an unverified standard as a fact
- Automatically approve or publish a tender
- Replace the final decision of a procurement officer or BIS expert

Every standard number shown to the user must exist in the verified standards database and have an official source.

If no sufficiently reliable result exists, the system must say:

“No verified recommendation found. Expert review is required.”
